Why symptoms get missed

Vision is a brain process, not just an eye process.

About 80% of how you take in the world travels through your visual system. When the brain is injured by a concussion, a stroke or a developmental difference, vision is often affected even when the eyes themselves are healthy and a standard exam reads “20/20.”

That is why the following are frequently overlooked:

  • Headaches or exhaustion after reading or screen time
  • Dizziness, motion sensitivity, or feeling “off balance”
  • Light sensitivity that sunglasses don't fix
  • Words swimming, doubling, or losing your place on the page
  • Difficulty in busy visual places such as grocery stores, traffic and crowds

If any of these sound familiar to you or someone you love, they are not “just stress,” and not something you have to live with. They can be measured objectively, and treated.

What to expect

Your first visit, without the waiting-room stress.

Our intake is designed around accessibility, which makes it easier for everyone. A neuro-visual evaluation runs one to two hours, much longer than a routine exam, because we are assessing how your whole visual system works rather than checking a prescription.
