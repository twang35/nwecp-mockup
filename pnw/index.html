Why symptoms get missed

Vision is a brain process, not just an eye process.

About 80% of how you take in the world travels through your visual system. After a concussion, a stroke or with a developmental difference, vision is often affected even when the eyes are healthy and a standard exam reads 20/20.

  • Headaches or exhaustion after reading or screen time
  • Dizziness, motion sensitivity or feeling off balance
  • Light sensitivity that sunglasses don't fix
  • Words swimming, doubling or losing your place
  • Trouble in busy places: stores, traffic, crowds

These are real, measurable and treatable. Evaluations run one to two hours because we assess the whole visual system, not just a prescription.
